Land use

Zoning Board of Adjustment

Decides whether a property owner may depart from the borough’s zoning rules. An approved departure is called a variance.

What it can and can’t decide

Can decide
  • Variances from rules like setbacks, height and lot coverage
  • Certain use variances, which need a stronger case
  • Appeals of the zoning officer’s decisions
Can’t decide
  • What the zoning rules say. That is the Mayor and Council’s job, advised by the Planning Board
  • The borough budget or tax rates
