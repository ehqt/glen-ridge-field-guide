Quick checklist
  • Read the agenda and find your item
  • Confirm the time and place the day before
  • Write down your name, address and two main points
  • Arrive a few minutes early
Can’t make it?
Minutes are published after a meeting, and you can usually email a comment to the board secretary or clerk ahead of time. Each institution page lists the contact.
